02 — About

Two trades, held to one line.

Evergreen & Stone began in 2004 with a mason and a horticulturist who kept arguing about the same properties. The argument turned out to be the method: plantings drawn around the stone, stone set to serve the plantings.

Twenty-one seasons later we still run one crew per property, one foreman from first stake to final walk-through, and no subcontracted hardscape. Everything we install, we maintain.
